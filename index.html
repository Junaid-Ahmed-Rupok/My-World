01 / About

Who I Am

I'm a Causal ML Researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from RUET (Thesis Grade: A+). My work focuses on developing principled methods for Causal Machine Learning and Trustworthy AI — building systems that reason about cause-and-effect while ensuring fairness, robustness, interpretability, and reproducibility.

I'm currently a Research Assistant at CAIR, Southeast University, Dhaka, and applying for fully funded PhD programs for Fall 2027. I've won two Best Paper Awards (APMEE 2025, SPECTRA 2026) and have 15+ publications across journals, IEEE/Springer conferences, and book chapters.

Beyond research, I'm a published poet, former radio jockey, lyricist, and composer — creative work that informs the clarity and narrative depth I bring to communicating complex ideas.
